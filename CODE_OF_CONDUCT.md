# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The FFM Sentinel project welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels. We aim to keep collaboration constructive, inclusive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept that people may have different experience levels and viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through available GitHub contact options. Include enough context to understand the concern while avoiding unnecessary exposure of private information.

Maintainers should review reports fairly and may remove content or restrict participation where necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and interactions related to the project.
